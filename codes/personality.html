My Strongest Personality Traits

Calm

One of my strongest personality traits is being calm. I try not to panic when I am faced with difficult situations. In my academic life, being calm helps me think clearly when completing assignments, studying, or dealing with stressful situations.

Determined

I am also a determined person. When I have a goal that I really want to achieve, I try to continue working towards it even when things become difficult. This helps me to keep trying when I experience challenges with schoolwork.

Lazy

Laziness is a trait that can sometimes affect me negatively. There are times when I delay starting schoolwork even when I know that it needs to be completed. Recognizing this weakness helps me understand that I need to improve my time management and become more disciplined.

LLMs: A tool, not a Cruthch

Large Language models such as chatGPT, claude and copilot can support students when they are used as leanring tools. They can help explain different concepts and give students another way of approching information they may not initially understand.

In computing, these tools can be useful when learning a programming concept or trying to understand an error. Instead of simply copying an answer, a student can ask for an explanation and then apply that knowledge independently.

However, depending completely on an LLM can negatively affect learning. Students still need to practise, research, think critically and verify information. AI-generated information may also contain errors, so its responses should not automatically be treated as correct.
