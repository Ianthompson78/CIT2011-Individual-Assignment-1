7 Cures for a Lean Purse

This chater introduces seven partical habits that help people improve their financial lives. The message is simple: spend less tham you earn, save consistently, and continue developing valuable skills.

Key Points

  • Save at least 10% of everything you earn.
  • Control unnecessary spending
  • Invest your savings so they grow
  • Prepare for emergencies and retirement
  • Protect yor money from risky investment
